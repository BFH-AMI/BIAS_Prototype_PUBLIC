NTNU Recruitment Positions: Hiring-Relevant Guidance

Extracted directly from NTNU's Guidelines for Recruitment Positions (2025)

General Recruitment Principles

As a rule, all teaching, research and researcher positions must be publicly advertised. This applies to:

permanent positions,
temporary positions,
all funding sources.

Everyone involved in recruitment must follow rules on impartiality. Individuals must not participate if they have:

close ties to applicants,
close collaboration with applicants (e.g. supervision or co-publication),
particular advantages or disadvantages resulting from the assessment.

All participants in recruitment processes are subject to confidentiality obligations.

Purpose of Recruitment Positions
Doctoral Research Fellow (PhD Candidate)

A doctoral research fellowship position is intended to:

lead to attainment of a doctoral degree,
contribute to qualification for careers in higher education,
contribute to qualification for careers in research institutions,
contribute to qualification for careers in other sectors where research competence is required.

For appointment:

admission to a doctoral programme, or
a binding agreement on admission,

is required.

Postdoctoral Fellow

The purpose of a postdoctoral position is to:

develop a research profile,
develop competence qualifying the employee for an associate professor position,
provide competence relevant to future career development.

For appointment:

a doctoral degree is required.
Announcement of Positions

Recruitment positions must normally:

be publicly advertised,
be announced internally.

The advertisement text must:

specify all requirements associated with the position,
follow NTNU guidelines and templates,
be prepared by the Head of Department.

For positions requiring project descriptions, longer application deadlines should be considered.

If:

no qualified applicants apply,
qualification requirements must be substantially changed,
a long time has passed since advertisement,
circumstances surrounding the position have significantly changed,

the position must be re-advertised.

Assessment Process

For all recruitment positions except certain student research assistant appointments, the following are required:

academic assessment,
interview,
reference checks.

The process includes two distinct assessments:

Academic Assessment

An academic committee evaluates applicants' academic qualifications relative to:

the advertisement text,
the qualification requirements of the position.

Normally, two academic staff members with at least associate professor qualifications provide written assessments.

Assessment of Personal Suitability

A recruitment group evaluates:

personal suitability,
interview performance,
references.

The recruitment group normally includes:

the immediate manager,
academic staff,
HR representatives.
Interview Requirements and Inclusion Measures

If qualified applicants indicate that they have:

a disability,
a CV gap,
a non-Western immigrant background,

at least one applicant from each applicable group must be invited to interview before recommendation is made.

This requirement concerns interview inclusion and does not specify selection outcomes.

Evaluation Criteria for Postdoctoral Applicants

For postdoctoral positions, emphasis shall be placed on:

documented academic qualifications,
project description (when required),
project quality,
personal suitability.
Evaluation Criteria for PhD Applicants

For doctoral research fellowship positions, emphasis shall be placed on:

qualifications for completing the doctoral education,
documented qualifications,
project description (when required),
project quality,
personal suitability.

The assessment is stated to be primarily based on documented qualifications and project quality together with personal suitability.

Recommendation Process

The Head of Department is the recommending authority.

Recommendations are based on:

an overall assessment,
applicant qualifications,
personal suitability,
the advertisement text,
academic assessment,
interviews,
reference checks.

At least two references must be obtained for each applicant being considered for recommendation.

Ranking Expectations

If several qualified applicants exist:

normally three applicants should be recommended and ranked,
a justification must be provided for the ranking.

Applicants who were interviewed shall be informed of:

their place in the recommendation,
their right to access the academic assessment of their own qualifications after appointment decisions.
Qualifications Emphasized in Recruitment Positions
PhD Candidates

NTNU explicitly evaluates whether applicants have qualifications for successfully completing doctoral education.

Relevant evidence includes:

academic qualifications,
project quality,
research potential,
personal suitability.
Postdoctoral Candidates

NTNU explicitly evaluates whether applicants possess qualifications that support development toward an associate professor career.

Relevant evidence includes:

research qualifications,
project quality,
personal suitability,
capacity for academic development.
Career Development Expectations

For both PhD candidates and postdoctoral fellows:

an individual career plan must be established,
career goals and measures must be specified,
career guidance must be available,
plans are followed up in annual employee interviews.

The career plan should include:

career goals,
measures to achieve those goals,
language training needs,
educational competence needs.
Career-Enhancing Activities Recognized by NTNU

Relevant career-enhancing work includes:

teaching,
supervision,
examination work,
training in research infrastructure,
language training,
pedagogical training,
HSE training,
dissemination activities,
exhibition and collection work,
research grant writing,
organizing academic conferences,
clinical activities,
participation in research projects,
academic development work,
consultancy work,
analysis work,
innovation activities,
dissemination projects,
internships,
collaboration with public-sector actors,
collaboration with private-sector actors,
elected positions,
committee work.

Career-enhancing work is explicitly defined as work beyond the research project itself that contributes to future career development.

Internationalization and Mobility

For postdoctoral fellows, NTNU states that candidates should normally:

have spent at least one year at another institution before employment, or
demonstrate extensive experience from academic environments outside NTNU.

If this requirement is not met, compensation should occur through:

stays abroad,
other measures providing experience from external academic environments.

NTNU further states that postdoctoral fellows should:

conduct research at an international level,
gain experience with research proposal writing,
receive training in research management.
Language Expectations

If a doctoral research fellow or postdoctoral fellow does not possess Norwegian, Swedish or Danish proficiency at level A2 upon appointment:

NTNU must offer free Norwegian language training,
the goal is achievement of at least A2-level proficiency.

Language training:

should be incorporated into career planning,
does not entitle the employee to contract extensions.
Personal Suitability

Personal suitability is explicitly part of recruitment decisions.

Assessment of personal suitability occurs through:

interviews,
reference checks,
recruitment-group evaluation.

Personal suitability is considered alongside academic qualifications and project quality rather than as a standalone criterion.

Overall Selection Principle

Recruitment decisions are based on an overall assessment of:

documented qualifications,
project quality,
qualifications for completing the position's objectives (e.g. doctoral education),
personal suitability,
evidence gathered through interviews,
evidence gathered through reference checks,
alignment with the requirements stated in the advertisement text.

For PhD recruitment specifically, NTNU emphasizes selecting applicants who demonstrate the strongest qualifications for successfully completing doctoral education.
