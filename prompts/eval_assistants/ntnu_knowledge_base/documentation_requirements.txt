NTNU PhD Admission and Foreign Education Documentation Requirements

Extracted directly from NTNU's "Documentation requirements for education obtained outside Norway"

General Eligibility for PhD Admission

To be admitted to a PhD programme at NTNU, applicants must normally hold:

a master's degree.

After special consideration, the Faculty may approve other comparable qualifications as a basis for admission.

For PhDs in artistic research, equivalent competence in the creative or performing arts may also be considered.

Applicants in the final stage of a master's programme may apply, but cannot begin the PhD position until all qualification requirements for the master's degree have been fulfilled.

Recognition of Foreign Education

Education obtained outside Norway must:

be accredited in the country where it was completed,
be recognized as higher education according to the standards of the country of study.

NTNU notes that significant differences between educational systems may result in only part of a foreign education being recognized as equivalent to Norwegian higher education.

Documentation Requirements for All Applicants with Foreign Education

Applicants must provide complete documentation covering their educational background.

Required documentation includes:

Bachelor's Degree
Final certificate
Degree/diploma/authorization
Transcript of grades

All documents must be officially approved in the country of study.

Master's Degree
Final certificate
Degree/diploma/authorization
Transcript of grades

All documents must be officially approved in the country of study.

Additional Higher Education

If applicable:

diploma(s)
transcript(s) of grades
Diploma Supplement

Applicants must provide a diploma supplement when their academic credentials include one.

Grading Scale Information

Applicants must provide documentation explaining the grading scale if it is not clearly shown on the transcript.

Additional Supporting Materials

Applicants are required to submit:

CV
references
testimonials
Translation Requirements

Academic credentials must be accompanied by official translations unless the original documents are in:

Norwegian,
Danish,
Swedish,
English.

Accepted translations include only those completed by:

government-authorized translators,
public interpreting services in Norway,
embassies.

NTNU does not accept translations performed by:

applicants themselves,
private individuals acting in a personal capacity.

A copy of the original document must accompany every translation.

Verification Requirements

NTNU requires verification of academic credentials from certain countries.

Verification means that educational documents must be confirmed as approved by the educational institution.

Applicants are responsible for arranging this verification themselves.

Applicants Near Completion of a Master's Degree

Applicants who have not yet completed their master's degree may submit:

a provisional transcript of grades

for assessment purposes.

However:

a completed master's degree is required for admission to a PhD programme,
a completed master's degree is required before starting the PhD position.
Recognition of Foreign Qualifications

Applicants with foreign higher education may apply for formal recognition of their qualifications through:

the Norwegian Directorate for Higher Education and Skills (HKDIR).

Recognition obtained through HKDIR may be submitted together with the PhD application.

Hiring-Relevant Implications

The document indicates that, for applicants educated outside Norway, evaluation may include verification of:

accreditation status of institutions,
completeness of educational documentation,
authenticity of academic credentials,
equivalence of qualifications to Norwegian higher education,
fulfillment of master's-level admission requirements.

The document does not provide ranking criteria for candidate selection, project evaluation, research potential, publication assessment, or personal suitability. It focuses exclusively on eligibility, documentation, verification, and qualification recognition requirements for admission to NTNU PhD programmes.
