NTNU Hiring-Relevant Policies and Principles

Extracted directly from NTNU's Development Plan for Gender Equality and Diversity 2023–2025

Recruitment and Hiring

NTNU's strategy includes the following development goals:

Improve gender balance in top-level academic positions.
Have an ambitious recruitment policy and effective procedures that ensure quality in hiring.
Strengthen international recruitment of outstanding researchers and increase the international mobility of employees.

The Equality and Anti-Discrimination Act requires NTNU to make active, targeted and systematic efforts to promote equality, prevent discrimination and promote inclusion.

Equality is defined as equal status, equal opportunities and equal rights. Accessibility and adaptations are prerequisites for equality.

Equality and diversity involve respecting and appreciating visible and invisible differences between people, including:

gender
age
ethnicity
affiliation to a group
religion
functional diversity
life experiences
cultural background
sexual orientation
different insights
level of education
work experience
competence
interests
family situation
experience as a minority
belonging to an under-represented group in a community.

It is important to take advantage of diversity as a resource in teaching, research and innovation.

Gender Balance in Recruitment

NTNU states that a diverse university helps to enrich knowledge production and that, in order to fulfil its social mission, the university must recruit managers and employees of all genders and backgrounds.

Women are under-represented in top-level academic positions, and targeted efforts must therefore be made to recruit more women to top-level positions.

NTNU states that this will be achieved by:

improving the quality of hiring processes,
pursuing active career development,
working actively to create an inclusive working environment for everyone, especially under-represented groups.
Goals

NTNU must strive to:

achieve gender balance,
counter discrimination based on gender in recruitment and appointment,
counter discrimination based on gender in salary determination,
counter discrimination based on gender in career development for staff and managers.

NTNU will make active efforts to increase the proportion of women in top-level academic positions.

Recruitment Actions

NTNU will:

ensure good candidates and greater diversity among applicants by using measures such as search committees,
emphasize skills and knowledge about equality, inclusion and diversity in the recruitment of new managers.

Education, experience and results must be given equal weight for women and men in new appointments.

The principle of equal pay for equal work must be followed in the appointment of staff and in pay negotiations.

Units must work actively to create conditions that make it possible to combine an academic career and family life.

Diversity and Inclusion in Recruitment

NTNU states that diversity enriches the university and that diversity presents opportunities and challenges for recruitment, inclusion and organizational development.

NTNU will work systematically and strategically with diversity and inclusion in recruitment and management at all levels.

NTNU will strive to ensure that under-represented groups are recruited to management positions.

Units are expected to:

understand what forms of diversity are present or absent,
identify under-represented groups or perspectives,
explore ways to recruit people from diverse backgrounds,
assess measures needed to achieve inclusion of under-represented groups.
Goal

NTNU will be an open, safe and inclusive place to work and study, where staff and students:

feel they belong,
are important in the community,
have opportunities for active participation and co-determination.
Recruitment Action

NTNU's recruitment processes must have an inclusive approach to a wide variety of applicants.

NTNU will actively combat racism.

International Recruitment

NTNU aims to be an attractive university for employees and students from all over the world.

Diversity of perspectives and cultures is considered enriching for the university.

Integration into academic and study environments is considered important for well-being and the opportunity to succeed in studies, research and teaching activities.

Goal

NTNU will create the conditions for staff and students who are recruited from other countries to thrive and experience mastery.

Actions
Departments will designate mentors for international employees during their first year.
Faculties should carry out introductory programmes for all international employees.
Management training will emphasize support for international employees and tailored practical measures to assist them.

"International employees and students" refers to employees and students who are recruited from countries outside Norway.

Functional Diversity

NTNU states that people with disability or functional variations possess specialized skills and experience that NTNU needs.

Adaptations and inclusion are important in order to safeguard and promote inclusion in study programmes and working life.

Goal

NTNU will create conditions that contribute to well-being and mastery for staff and students with disability.

Actions

NTNU will improve the skills of its managers in adaptation and support for people with disability and ensure effective procedures for adaptation and accessibility.

Gender Identity, Sexuality and Gender Expression

NTNU aims to be an organization that shows respect for all people regardless of gender identity, sexuality and gender expression.

Goal

NTNU will be an open and inclusive organization where no one is subjected to discrimination or harassment based on gender identity, sexuality or gender expression.

Discrimination, Harassment and Abuse of Power

NTNU has zero tolerance for:

sexual harassment,
harassment,
sexual abuse,
racism,
other abuse of power.
Goal

Discrimination, harassment, racism and sexual harassment of staff and students must not occur at NTNU.

Managers have a duty to act when they are informed of unacceptable behaviour.

Everyone with management responsibility must:

set clear boundaries,
signal clear expectations of mutual respect,
promote good collegial behaviour and cooperation.
Responsibilities of Leaders

Gender equality and diversity are managerial responsibilities.

Leaders at NTNU must have a conscious attitude to:

equality,
inclusion,
diversity.

Skills and knowledge about gender equality and diversity must be included in leadership development programmes.

The Committee for Gender Equality and Diversity advises the Rector on measures that contribute to:

enabling NTNU to reflect diversity in the population in terms of gender, age, functional needs, ethnic background and sexual orientation,
increased knowledge and strengthened development of gender equality and diversity,
planning and implementation of measures to increase gender equality and diversity at NTNU.
