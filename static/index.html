BIAS Project Prototype

This prototype combines Case-Based Reasoning (CBR), large-language-model evaluation, pairwise head-to-head comparison and structured human ratings to arrive at a shortlist of candidates — while surfacing fairness and policy signals throughout the process.

CBR System

Scores each candidate by comparing them to the nearest similar cases in a labelled case base. The final CBR score is a similarity-weighted average of historical shortlist / longlist / reject labels.

How CBR scores are computed →

Strength Transparent, tunable weights and explainable neighbour evidence.

Watch out Label bias in the case base propagates to new scores; weight choices can encode hidden assumptions.

LLM Evaluator

An LLM reads every candidate summary alongside the job ad and independently produces a shortlist and longlist. Its output seeds the head-to-head pool but does not replace human judgment.

Strength Captures semantic fit that structured features miss.

Watch out Prompt sensitivity; biases in pre-training data; opaque reasoning unless the rationale is inspected.

Head-to-Head Comparison

You compare candidates in pairwise rounds, section by section. Each decision updates a live score through a lightweight online learning rule, progressively separating strong candidates from borderline ones.

Strength Forces explicit, documented comparisons; section-level choices are logged.

Watch out Order effects and fatigue can influence decisions; too few rounds leave the ranking under-determined.

Fairness Assistant

Runs alongside the ratings stage. It generates an initial fairness review per candidate, answers follow-up questions and audits consistency across the full set of ratings when you submit.

Strength Surfaces attribute-based risks and rating inconsistencies before a decision is locked in.

Watch out The assistant flags risks — it does not make decisions. Its output should prompt reflection, not be taken as ground truth.

NTNU Hiring Assistant

A policy-aware assistant grounded in NTNU HR, recruitment and PhD-admission documents. It checks eligibility, documentation requirements and procedural obligations for each candidate and answers targeted policy questions.

See NTNU knowledge-base documents →

Strength Keeps institutional requirements visible throughout the evaluation, not only at the end.

Watch out Only covers documents in the knowledge base; always verify against current official sources for binding decisions.
